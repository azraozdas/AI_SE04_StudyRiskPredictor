# Problem Analysis

## Problem
Students have difficulty identifying which courses, assignments, or deadlines are risky. Because of this, they may manage their study time poorly and miss important deadlines.

## Target Users
University or high school students who want to manage study workload better.

## Why This Problem Matters
Students often study without knowing which task needs the most attention. An AI system can help them understand academic risk earlier.

## Proposed Solution
The system analyzes study hours, attendance, deadlines, and past grades. It predicts whether a student is at low, medium, or high academic risk and gives personalized study recommendations.

## Inputs
- Study hours
- Attendance
- Assignment deadlines
- Past grades
- Course/workload information

## Outputs
- Risk prediction
- Workload level
- Study recommendation
